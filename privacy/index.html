Recovery Day Alberta is organized by The Last Door Recovery Society (“Last Door”, “we”). This policy explains what personal information we collect through recoverydayalberta.com, why, and how we protect it. We follow Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

What we collect

  • Vendors and sponsors: organization name and address, contact name, email and phone, booth and sponsorship choices, invoices and payments, and documents you upload (for example insurance or food permits).
  • Volunteers: name, email, phone, age group, parent or guardian details for volunteers aged 14–17, preferences and availability, emergency contact, t-shirt size and any accessibility needs you choose to share.
  • Staff accounts: name, email and a record of changes made (audit log).

We do not collect credit card numbers. Card payments are made on Last Door’s own payment page.

Why we use it

  • To run Recovery Day: review applications, assign booths and volunteer shifts, send invoices and receipts, and contact you about the event.
  • For safety on the day, including emergency contacts.
  • To keep financial records required by law.
  • Only if you opt in: to send news about future Recovery Day events, and to use photos or video of you to promote the event. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the website (listed below), and when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Information is stored with Supabase in its Canada (Central) region and emails are sent through Resend. Uploaded documents are private: only you and authorized Last Door staff can open them, using links that expire after a few minutes. Staff accounts use two-factor sign-in.

How long we keep it

  • Volunteer information: deleted 24 months after the event.
  • Uploaded documents: deleted 12 months after the event.
  • Invoices, payments, receipts and refunds: kept for 7 years, as required for charity financial records.

Your choices

  • Vendors can download a copy of their information from the portal (Profile & privacy → Download my data).
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to stop sending you news, at any time. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link.

Contact

Questions or requests: community@lastdoor.org, or write to The Last Door Recovery Society, 323 8th Street, New Westminster, BC V3M 3R3. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
